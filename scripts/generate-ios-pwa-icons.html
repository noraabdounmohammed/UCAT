🍎 Generate iOS PWA Icons

iOS requires PNG icons for PWA installation. This tool generates all required sizes.

⚠️ iOS PWA Requirements:
  • PNG format only (no SVG)
  • Apple touch icon: 180x180px
  • Manifest icons: 192x192px and 512x512px
  • Favicon: 32x32px
