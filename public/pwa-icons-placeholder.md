# PWA Icons Needed

Please create the following icon files in the `/public` folder:

## Required Icons:

1. **pwa-192x192.png** (192x192 pixels)
   - Your app logo/icon
   - PNG format
   - Square, centered design

2. **pwa-512x512.png** (512x512 pixels)
   - Your app logo/icon
   - PNG format
   - Square, centered design
   - Same design as 192x192, just larger

3. **apple-touch-icon.png** (180x180 pixels)
   - For iOS home screen
   - PNG format

4. **favicon.ico** (32x32 pixels)
   - Browser tab icon
   - ICO format

## Design Guidelines:
- **Simple and recognizable** at small sizes
- **High contrast** for visibility
- **No text** (except maybe a single letter)
- **Solid background** (no transparency for some platforms)
- **Centered design** with padding

## Color Scheme:
Based on your app's theme:
- Primary: #1c1917 (stone-900)
- Background: #fafaf9 (stone-50)
- Accent: Your choice (blue, green, etc.)
